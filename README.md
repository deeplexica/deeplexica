## ◈ About Deep Lexica

**Deep Lexica** focuses on **artificial intelligence, AI automation, machine learning and deep learning** to solve real-world challenges. We explore practical ways to turn complex problems into intelligent, measurable and reliable solutions.

Our areas of interest include **LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, predictive analytics and intelligent workflows**. Our approach values thoughtful problem-solving, experimentation, evaluation and continuous improvement.
